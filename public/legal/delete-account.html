Delete your AMZY cloud account

This verifies the Telegram account linked to your AMZY cloud data, then deletes the cloud profile, saved channels, favorites, playlists, device key, and pending verification records. A minimal account identifier is retained for up to eight days to revoke old sessions, then automatically purged. It does not delete your Telegram account or erase local AMZY downloads/Telegram data on a device.

You will confirm ownership using a one-time Telegram bot link.
