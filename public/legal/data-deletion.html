Request deletion of AMZY data

To remove individual synced channels or favorites, open AMZY Profile and remove the selected item. To request deletion of other cloud profile data without deleting your Telegram account, email service@chakrabortycompliance.in from the email address you want us to use for the reply. Include your Telegram numeric ID, the data categories you want deleted, and the word “AMZY” in the subject. Do not send your Telegram password, login code, or two-step verification password.

We will verify the request through the Telegram account linked to the AMZY cloud profile before deleting data. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Some minimal account identifiers may remain for up to eight days only to revoke existing sessions, after which they are automatically purged.

To delete the whole AMZY cloud account, use the account deletion page. This does not erase your Telegram account, local Telegram session, or downloaded files from your device.
